Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data may be processed when you use Smart Web Utility.

Last updated: September 27, 2026.

Browser-side tools

Many tools process your input locally in your browser. Examples include JSON formatting, Base64 conversion, text counters, UUID generation, hashing, case conversion and similar utilities. For these tools, the content you enter is not intentionally sent to Smart Web Utility's application server for processing.

Network and website diagnostic tools

Tools that inspect a domain, URL, DNS record, SSL certificate, sitemap or webpage must send the public target you provide to the service performing the requested lookup. Most network tools use Smart Web Utility server endpoints. The homepage multi-country DNS check sends the public hostname directly from your browser to Globalping so distributed probes can perform the requested DNS measurement. Do not submit private, confidential or unauthorized internal addresses.

Technical logs

Our hosting infrastructure may process or retain standard technical information such as IP address, user agent, timestamps, requested URLs, response codes and security events. These logs may be used for reliability, abuse prevention, debugging and security.

Analytics and advertising

Smart Web Utility uses Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate site usage, such as page views, device categories, traffic sources and general interaction patterns. The Google Analytics measurement ID currently used by the site is G-TKF8FWKQN6. For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Analytics storage is configured to remain denied unless a consent mechanism records a different choice. When consent is denied, Google tags may operate in a restricted, cookieless mode.

If Google AdSense is enabled, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads. Google advertising cookies can enable Google and its partners to serve ads based on a user's visits to Smart Web Utility and/or other websites.

Users can manage or opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. If additional third-party advertising vendors or ad networks are enabled, this policy will be updated to identify or link to information about those providers and the choices they offer.

Consent for Europe and related regions

For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we will use a Google-certified consent management platform where required for Google advertising. The consent flow may ask for choices relating to cookies or local storage, ad personalization, measurement and the collection, sharing or use of personal data for advertising. Depending on the user's choices and applicable rules, personalized, non-personalized or limited ads may be served.

Third-party services

Some network tools rely on public DNS or internet infrastructure providers to complete requested lookups. Their systems may receive technical request data necessary to answer the query. The homepage global DNS checker uses Globalping distributed probes; when you run that check, Globalping receives the public hostname and the browser/network request information needed to provide the measurement.

Data security

We use reasonable technical measures intended to reduce misuse of network-facing endpoints, including public-target validation and limits on outbound requests. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can avoid entering personal or confidential information into tools. When consent controls are available, you can use them to manage eligible advertising and analytics choices.

Changes

We may update this policy as the site, hosting, analytics or advertising setup changes. The date above will be updated when material changes are published.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the information published on our Contact page.
