Online word counter with live text statistics

It also adds reading and speaking estimates, unique-word count, average word length and optional target progress for a more complete writing-length workflow.

There is no universal SEO word-count target

A higher count does not automatically make a page more useful or more likely to rank. Write enough to satisfy the search intent accurately, remove repetition and keep important answers easy to find.

What this word counter counts

Words are counted as non-whitespace sequences, characters use the browser string length, characters without spaces remove whitespace, sentences are estimated from common ending punctuation, paragraphs are blocks separated by blank lines, and lines are separated by line breaks. Different editors can use different counting rules, so small differences between tools are possible.

Reading time, speaking time and word-goal progress

The live estimates use explicit defaults of 200 words per minute for reading and 130 words per minute for speaking. They are planning estimates rather than promises: technical material, unfamiliar vocabulary, pauses and presentation style can change the real duration. Set an optional target to see words remaining or how far the draft is over the target.

Unique words and average word length

Unique-word count lowercases tokens and removes punctuation only from the beginning and end before comparison. Average word length uses those normalized tokens and Unicode code points. These are descriptive writing statistics, not readability grades or quality scores.

Word count and SEO

Word count is a descriptive measurement, not a ranking target. Repeating phrases or padding copy to hit a length can make a page less useful. For SEO content, satisfy the search intent accurately, cover the entities and questions readers need, and remove repetition rather than optimizing toward a synthetic density score.
