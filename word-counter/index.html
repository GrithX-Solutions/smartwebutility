About this tool

Use the word counter for writing, SEO copy, assignments, captions and content checks. All counting happens locally in your browser.

Count words and text structure instantly

The Word Counter updates six measurements as you type: words, total characters, characters without whitespace, sentences, paragraphs and lines. The calculation runs locally in your browser.

Writing limits

Use the live word count when an assignment, article, brief or submission has a stated minimum or maximum length.

Editing structure

Sentence, paragraph and line counts can help you spot unusually dense blocks while editing documentation, marketing copy or long-form content.

Character context

The total and no-space counts are useful when a platform applies exact field limits rather than word limits.

Private by design

The pasted text is counted in your browser and does not require an upload to Smart Web Utility.

There is no universal SEO word-count target

A higher count does not automatically make a page more useful or more likely to rank. Write enough to satisfy the search intent accurately, remove repetition and keep important answers easy to find.

What this word counter counts

Words are counted as non-whitespace sequences, characters use the browser string length, characters without spaces remove whitespace, sentences are estimated from common ending punctuation, paragraphs are blocks separated by blank lines, and lines are separated by line breaks. Different editors can use different counting rules, so small differences between tools are possible.

Word count

Use the word total for essays, articles, briefs and submissions with a stated word limit.

Character count

Use total characters or characters without spaces when a form, metadata field or publishing platform measures text length by characters.

Sentence and paragraph count

These structural counts help you review dense copy, long blocks and pacing while editing.

Line count

Line totals are useful for plain-text notes, scripts, lists, configuration snippets and other newline-sensitive content.

Estimate reading time and speaking time from the word count

A word count can also be used to estimate reading or speaking duration: divide the total words by the reading or speaking rate you want to model. Treat the result as a planning estimate because reading speed changes with language, difficulty, familiarity and the audience, while speaking pace changes with pauses and presentation style.

Word count, keyword frequency and SEO

Word count and keyword frequency are descriptive measurements, not ranking targets. Repeating a phrase to hit a density percentage can make copy less useful. For SEO content, cover the topic with the terms and entities readers need, answer the main task clearly, and use headings and internal links to make related information easy to find.
